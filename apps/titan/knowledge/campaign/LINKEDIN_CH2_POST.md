I've spent the last several weeks publishing
what I call the "Growth Paradox" series —

Here's what I found when I read the actual budget:

**Before Jacksonville spends a single dollar on:**
- Filling a pothole
- Hiring a new officer

- Building a park in a new neighborhood

**Nearly 70 cents of every dollar is already committed.**

The pension crisis: $2.9-3.2 BILLION unfunded.
Every year: $280-320M just to service that legacy.
The JEA transfer: ~$115M/year from your utility bill
to the general fund. (Did you know that?)

- Nashville: ~$2,100/resident
- Charlotte: ~$1,950/resident  
- Austin: ~$2,400/resident
- Jacksonville: ~$1,700/resident

And a higher percentage of Jacksonville's
$1,700 goes to legacy costs that produce
ZERO current-year services.

More revenue coming in.
